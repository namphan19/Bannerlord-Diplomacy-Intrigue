## 1. User story

> **As** the lead,
> **I want** the phase's acceptance scenario played through once in a real campaign, and AI
> espionage measured over years,
> **so that** I accept Phase 3 on evidence, as I did Phase 2, and know what goes to Phase 4.

## 2. Why

Each piece of Phase 3 was verified live on its own (design 03 §10): a bribe lands and the house
rises with the rebels, an exposure grants `EspionageExposed`, trust falls, the handler is taken. The
**chain** has never been run in one campaign, and the AI half has never run long:

| Not yet seen | Where it is recorded |
|---|---|
| The acceptance scenario as one story | ROADMAP.md, Phase 3 acceptance |
| AI espionage over years: networks, operations and exposures per year, and whether exposures start wars | design 03 §10, 3.6 check 5 |
| Whether counter-intelligence at the AI's budgets pushes back enough | design 03 §10, 3.3 "By hand, not measured" |
| The "caught in our realm" list on the Intelligence tab with an entry in it | design 03 §10, 3.7 "Not verified" |
| Handler losses after 3.8 | 3.8 AC4 |

## 3. Scope

**In**
- **Part A, the scenario, live and driven by the player's own actions** (staging levers only to reach
  the starting state, never to force an outcome; every forced roll is said so in the report).
- **Part B, a long AI-only run** (the player outside every realm or in a minor role, as run 09/10
  did), at least **5 in-game years** on the real clock (`test_set_speed`), `scripts/play.ps1`
  without the GABS module for the unattended part (CLAUDE.md §1).
- A run report `docs/balance/run-11.md` in the shape of run-10.md, and the acceptance table.

**Out**
- Tuning. Numbers outside the bars below are reported with a recommendation; changing constants is
  a decision for the lead and a separate commit.
- Anything the long run shows about diplomacy or intrigue that is not caused by espionage. It goes in
  the report's "observed" section and in TODO.md.

## 4. Part A — the scenario

The player rules a realm, or is a vassal with a house network (decide which in ST-1; ruling is
simpler because the casus belli lands on the player's own realm).

| Step | The player does | What must be seen |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Posts a handler in a neighbouring realm with a shaky crown and builds the network through the Intelligence tab (`test_set_network` may stand in for the weeks of growth; say so) | Network, handler and odds on the tab agree with `diplomacy.networks` and `mission_odds` |
| A2 | Bribes the head of a **border** house (holds a fief adjacent to the player's realm) from the tab | On success: `foreign gold -20.0`, the house's band on the Encyclopedia page moves, `diplomacy.bribes` binds it |
| A3 | The internal war starts in that realm (on its own trigger, or `test_start_internal_war` if it does not come within the bribe's two years; say which) | The bought house rises with the rebels, with "bought with our gold" in the notice and the log |
| A4 | A second operation in a realm **at peace** with the player, which is **exposed** (unforced if possible: launch at low odds and repeat; if forced, say so) | The red notice; `EspionageExposed` held by the victim against the player's realm; trust -25; the handler a prisoner; network 0 |
| A5 | The player does nothing further | **The victim declares war on the player's realm within the claim's life, citing `EspionageExposed`**, through the AI's ordinary war decision. If it does not in the claim's years, that is the finding (see the bar below) |
| A6 | The player, as the victim of an AI operation (or in a second save), opens the Intelligence tab | The "caught in our realm" list shows the entry (closes 3.7's last unverified item) |

**Bar for Part A:** A1-A4 and A6 as described. **A5 is the acceptance criterion's second half**: if
the victim never declares, the report says why in terms of the war valuation (`diplomacy.war_value
<victim> | <player's realm>`, read weekly), and the lead decides whether the phase is accepted with
that gap or whether `EspionageExposed` needs more weight than its 0.85 legitimacy factor
(`CasusBelli.cs:88`).

## 5. Part B — the long run

Read from the log with `tools/analyse-log.py`, per year, and compared with run 09:

| Measure | Bar (UN-TUNED, a first statement of "working") |
|---|---|
| AI networks with a handler at year's end | at least half of the ruling houses that have a free member |
| AI network strength, median at year 2 | above 30 (the `SpreadDissent` floor) |
| AI operations launched per year, all realms | > 0 from year 2, every type the AI may choose seen at least once in 5 years |
| Exposures per year | reported, with the share that led to a war within the claim's life |
| Wars citing `EspionageExposed` | reported; **no bar**. Zero is a finding for the lead, not a pass |
| `handler_lost` per cause per year (3.8 R8) | `party` and `governor` at 0; `forced-party` reported, and above 3 a year goes to the lead (3.8 D2) |
| The player's house as a mark (3.10) | reported: how often the AI chose a member of it, the player hero, or the house for forged letters. In Part B the player is out of reach, so expect 0 there; Part A or a short attended session covers it |
| Counter-intelligence: realms ordering a budget, and the mean exposure chance against them | reported, against 3.3's hand figures (6.4% / 11.7%) |
| War share and mean war length | within run 10's range: espionage must not have moved diplomacy on its own |
| Errors / warnings in the mod log | 0 `ERROR` |

## 6. Acceptance criteria

**AC1** — Part A run, each step recorded with a screenshot or log line, every lever named.
**AC2** — Part B run for ≥ 5 in-game years, report written, every row of §5 filled.
**AC3** — An acceptance table in `docs/STATUS.md` and ROADMAP.md marks each Phase 3 item
verified live, verified by compile only, or not verified, as Phase 2's did, with what goes to Phase 4.
**AC4** — Every defect found is either fixed (with its own commit and a re-run of the step that
found it) or listed for the lead in TODO.md. Nothing is fixed silently.

## 8. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| An AI peace offer or other inquiry stops the clock mid-run (CLAUDE.md §2) | Keep the player out of every realm's throne in Part B; check `check_blockers` when the log goes quiet |
| The player hero dies of old age in a 5-year run | `diplomacy.test_set_player_age` before starting (test save only) |
| Part A's exposure never comes unforced | Allowed to force it, said so in the report. The acceptance is about what follows the exposure, not the roll |
| `tick_days`/`ai_week` used to shorten Part B | Not allowed: they do not move `CampaignTime.Now`, so claims never age and networks never grow on the real schedule (CLAUDE.md §2). Real clock only |
| The machine is on v1.5.3, players on v1.4.8 | Run the DLL `release.ps1` builds against the v1.4.8 refs, and say which DLL in the report |

## 9. ST-1 and ST-2, done 2026-10-02 (the tech lead)

**ST-1:** [run-11-runbook.md](../balance/run-11-runbook.md). The lead asked for one run that validates
everything at the end, so the runbook also gathers the checks 3.8-3.11 left open (its §3) ahead of
Parts A and B. Its §0 asks the lead four questions before the run; the first departs from §3 of this
story: Part B **through GABS, monitored**, because `test_set_speed` needs the bridge and `play.ps1` at
default speed takes about 2 h 20 min for five years.

**ST-2 needed code, which §0 of this story did not foresee.** The analyser could not read Part B
because the log did not hold it: espionage wrote only `handler_lost`. Added, with no save data:

| Record | What it carries | Part B row it answers |
|---|---|---|
| `[NETWORK]`, weekly, one per network | owner, whether it rules, target, handler, at post, strength, ceiling, budget, idle | handlers at year's end; median strength at year 2 |
| `[KINGDOM]` new fields | `counterIntel`, `counterBudget`, `counterSpent`; `spyHandlers`, `spyFree` (by `SpyNetworks.IsFreeToGo`, split out of `CanHandle` so the count and the AI's plan apply one rule); `rulerIsPlayer`; `warParties` | the denominator "ruling houses with a free member"; counter-intelligence; 3.8 AC5's party count, recorded for the first time |
| `mission_launched` | type, mark, `markPlayerHouse` / `markPlayer`, launch odds, the target's counter-intelligence | operations per year and type; exposure against each realm; the player's house as a mark |
| `mission_resolved` | outcome and `how`: `rolled`, `forced`, `offer`, `player-accepted` / `-refused`, `lapsed`, `no-handler`, `no-mark` | outcomes, with the AI's own rolls told apart from everything else |
| `espionage_exposed` | owner, offender realm, victim, claim and its life in days, handler captured, trust after | exposures per year; matched to the victim's `war_opened` inside the claim's life |

`tools/analyse-log.py` has an ESPIONAGE section that prints each row of §5 per run year with its bar,
and a LOG HEALTH section that counts `ERROR` lines. Tested on an old log (it says the records are
missing and moves on) and on a synthetic log holding every new record. **The mod side has not run in a
game**; run 11's §2.2 checks the records appear before anything else.

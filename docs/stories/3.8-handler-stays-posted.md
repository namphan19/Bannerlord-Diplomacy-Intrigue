## 1. User story

> **As** the ruler of an AI realm that has posted a handler abroad,
> **I want** that handler to stay on the network until I recall them,
> **so that** my network grows the way the player's does, under the same rules.

And for the player: an AI network that grows is a threat they can see coming and defend against.
Today it is a threat that never materialises.

## 2. Why

Run 09 measured it over two campaign years: **21 handler losses** (party 16, governor 3, recalled 1,
left the clan 1), the AI re-posting each time, and AI networks aimed at a realm at war ending near
**0**. The player's handler was never lost. Same rules, uneven outcome: vanilla's clan AI treats a
posted handler as an idle lord and gives them a job.

Run 09 also found the causes, with IL read on v1.5.3 and two live cases:

1. **Governor.** Vanilla *does* ask `ClanPoliticsModel.CanHeroBeGovernor`, and our override
   ([ModClanPoliticsModel.cs](../../src/DiplomacyIntrigue/GameModels/ModClanPoliticsModel.cs))
   refuses a handler. The hole is a hero **already travelling** to take up a governorship:
   `SpyNetworks.CanHandle` checks `GovernorOf` (still null in transit) but not `IsTraveling`. The mod
   teleports the hero abroad, and vanilla's queued governor teleport completes 3-5 real seconds later
   (Hajara, Simir).
2. **Party.** No `GameModel` answers "may this hero lead a party", so a handler is handed a party,
   the daily check releases them (`StillHandles`), and the network sits idle until next week's plan.
   `CampaignEvents.CanHeroLeadPartyEvent` is a veto the engine asks.
3. **Station.** `StillHandles` never checks that the handler is still in the target realm. Vanilla
   moves idle AI lords home: 7 of 7 AI handlers were outside their target on day 15, while their
   networks kept growing. That is a defect either way: the fiction says the handler is stationed
   abroad, and the code does not hold it to that.

Two documents say something run 09 disproved, and both are fixed here (CLAUDE.md §5):
design 03 §10 ("whether vanilla's AI governor assignment asks it is **not known**") and the
`NOT VERIFIED` paragraph in `ModClanPoliticsModel`'s header.

## 3. Scope

**In**
- `SpyNetworks.CanHandle`: refuse a hero in transit to a post, a fugitive, or a hero just released.
- A veto on a handler being given a party, through a campaign event, not Harmony.
- The station enforced: a veto on leaving the target realm, and a daily return (D1).
- The two document corrections above.
- Telemetry so 3.12 can count handler losses by cause.

**Out**
- Harmony, **by the lead's decision (D2)**. If the event vetoes do not hold on some path, that path
  is measured and reported (R5, AC4), not patched.
- What the AI does with its network (3.9, 3.10).
- The player's handler. It is already never lost; the new rules apply to it too, because they
  are rules about a handler, not about the AI.

## 4. Business rules

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | **Who may handle.** `CanHandle` refuses, in addition to today's checks, a hero who `IsTraveling` (on the way to a post, a governorship included), `IsFugitive`, or `IsReleased`. Each with a reason in the same style as the existing ones |
| R2 | **No party.** A hero who handles a network cannot be made a party leader: a listener on `CampaignEvents.CanHeroLeadPartyEvent` answers false for any hero `SpyNetworks.HandledBy` returns a network for. The player is asked the same question: a handler in the player's clan cannot be given a party either, until recalled. Same rule, not an AI exemption |
| R3 | **No governorship.** Unchanged: `ModClanPoliticsModel.CanHeroBeGovernor`. R1 closes the transit hole, which is the only way run 09 saw it beaten |
| R4 | **The station is enforced (D1).** A listener on `CampaignEvents.CanMoveToSettlementEvent` refuses a handler a settlement outside the target realm. A handler found outside the target realm by the daily check is sent back to the station (`SpyNetworks.StationFor`) and logged with cause `left-realm` in the telemetry, rather than released. If the station town changes hands, the handler is re-stationed to the target's new `StationFor` |
| R5 | **The residual, stated and not hidden.** Vanilla's second pass for a clan with no other free lord ignores the party veto (run 09 §4, from IL). If live checking shows handlers still lost on that path, the network is released as today and the cause is logged as `forced-party`. **No Harmony (D2)**: 3.12 measures it, and above 3 a year across all realms it goes back to the lead as a finding |
| R6 | **No throw crosses the engine.** Every listener catches, logs, and answers what vanilla would have answered |
| R7 | **Switchable with the pillar.** With espionage off in MCM, every listener answers as vanilla would, the same gate `ModClanPoliticsModel` already uses |
| R8 | **Each loss names its cause.** `SpyNetworks.Release` already takes a reason; add `Telemetry.Event("handler_lost", owner, target, hero, cause)` with a closed set of causes (`party`, `governor`, `prisoner`, `died`, `left-clan`, `became-head`, `recalled`, `forced-party`, `left-realm`), and teach `tools/analyse-log.py` to count it |

## 5. Acceptance criteria

**AC1 — A traveller is refused**
- A hero `IsTraveling` to a governorship: `CanHandle` returns false with "... is on the way to a
  post." `diplomacy.ai_espionage` shows the plan picking someone else, or nobody.

**AC2 — The party veto holds**
- A posted AI handler, three in-game weeks on the real clock (`test_set_speed`): no `handler_lost`
  with cause `party` or `governor`, on `di_36_espionage_test` and `di_run07_1104`.
- The player's own handler: the clan screen does not offer them a party (screenshot).

**AC3 — The station holds**
- On day 15 of AC2, every AI handler is in a settlement of its target realm
  (`diplomacy.networks` prints each handler's current settlement and realm; add that if it does not).

**AC4 — The residual is measured, not assumed**
- The 3.12 run reports `handler_lost` per cause per year. `forced-party` is reported separately, with
  a count. If it is above 3 a year across all realms, that is written up for the lead.

**AC5 — Nothing else moved**
- A clan with no handler gets parties and governors exactly as before: in a two-year run, the count
  of AI parties is within run 09's range (report both).
- `build.ps1`, `deploy.ps1` and `compile-check.sh` pass; the `Harmony patched N methods` line is
  unchanged (6).

**AC6 — Documents**
- design 03 §10's "not known" paragraph and `ModClanPoliticsModel`'s `NOT VERIFIED` paragraph say what
  run 09 found, with the date and the evidence, and the 3.6 entry in ROADMAP.md is updated.

## 7. Decisions

| # | Question | Answer |

| D1 | Is the handler's station enforced, or dropped from the fiction? | **Decided 2026-10-01 (lead): enforced** (R4). ## 8. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| A clan with one free lord, who is the handler, cannot field a party; vanilla's second pass overrides the veto | R5: measured (AC4), not hidden. The AI's plan already prefers a member with nothing else to do |
| The move veto traps a handler in a town that falls to a third realm | The daily check re-stations to `StationFor` of the target; a realm with no town releases the handler as today (`CanHandle`'s "holds no town" rule) |
| The event is asked on a path that must not be refused (a hero escaping captivity, a quest) | The listener answers only for a hero with a live network, and only the two questions above. AC5 checks that nothing else moved |

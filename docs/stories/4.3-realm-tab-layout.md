## 1. User story

> **As** a player who rules or serves a realm,
> **I want** the Realm tab to show my standing, wars, claims and agreements without any text
> drawn over other text,
> **so that** I can read what a treaty or a claim is worth without hunting for it.

> **As** a player who reads the game in German or Russian,
> **I want** the same tab to hold together when every label is a third longer,
> **so that** a translation does not turn the tab into a pile of overlapping lines.

## 2. What the lead saw (2026-10-04)

The lead's words: some regions of text overlap, the player has to scroll a lot, and there is a lot of
empty space. The screenshots show three separate faults, and they should not be fixed as one:

| # | Fault | Where | Seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Text drawn over text.** The "Spheres on the map" heading and its explanation run into the block below it, so two paragraphs print on the same lines | Left column, under our sphere | English, 1920x1080 |
| F2 | **A tall empty stretch inside the claims column.** The first claim group is followed by a blank area several hundred pixels high, and the next group (Battania's claims) sits at the very bottom, reached only by scrolling | Middle column, after the first group | Deutsch, scrolled; the first view of the English tab shows the same column shorter |
| F3 | **Scrolling while most of the panel is empty.** The three columns are different heights, so the longest one sets the scroll length and the other two leave blank space beside it | Whole tab | Both |

A fourth item the lead named in an earlier pass (TODO, "Kingdom-UI loose ends"): the Realm tab
**widens the tab strip**. It is in scope only if ST-1 shows it shares a cause with F1-F3.

**Out**

## 4. Business rules

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | **No text over text.** At every size the panel is shown at, no `TextWidget` overlaps another. A line that does not fit wraps or is cut by its own card; it never prints on a neighbour |
| R2 | **Height follows content, or is fixed on purpose.** A card is either `Fixed` height with its text proven to fit, or sized by its content. A `CoverChildren` widget holding a `StretchToParent` child is not allowed (UI-INTEGRATION §0b.1, §0b.4: this bit the same panel twice) |
| R3 | **A column never sets the height of its neighbours' blank space.** Where one column is much taller, the shorter ones are not left holding an empty strip next to it. How that is achieved is ST-2's decision |
| R4 | **Longest language is the test, not English.** A fix that holds only in English is not done. English is the shortest of the 13 |
| R5 | **Text is not shortened to make it fit.** Words are the lead's and the translators'; the layout makes room. (If a line truly cannot be fitted, report it; do not cut it.) |
| R6 | **No change to data or logic.** Same properties, same text, same order of information |

## 5. Acceptance criteria

**AC1 — No overlap**
- On `di_phase1_full` and one save with a vassal, a sphere and a live claim, no text overlaps another in
  the Realm tab, in English, `DE`, `RU` and `CNs`. Evidence is a screenshot of each, not a statement.

**AC2 — No wasted scroll**
- With the content of AC1 the tab needs no more scrolling than its longest column requires, and no
  blank area taller than one card sits between two blocks of content. Stated as a measured number in
  ST-1 and re-measured in ST-5.

**AC3 — Content unchanged**
- Every property the prefab bound before it binds after; every sentence reads as before (English
  character for character). `check-localization.ps1` passes, and the 4.1 golden file is unchanged.

**AC4 — Nothing else moves**
- The Diplomacy and Court tabs, the vanilla tabs and the tab strip render as before. If the strip
  widening is fixed, that is shown by a screenshot of the strip.

**AC5 — Honest about what was exercised**
- Each AC marked verified live, compile only, or not verified. Anything not clicked says so.

## 9. Findings

### 9a. ST-1 — the cause of each fault, measured

Measured on `di_phase1_full` with the player a vassal of Battania (a sphere, a live claim and a
war all present), v1.5.3. **Two resolutions were used**: the "before" numbers are 1920x1080,
the "after" numbers 1280x720 - `engine_config.txt` on this machine had been changed to 1280x720
by a session before this one, and the launcher does not restore it. The two sets are therefore
**not** comparable to each other; each is internally consistent. Restoring 1920x1080 through
`engine_config.txt` and restarting did not take effect, so D3 stands: the fix is verified at
1280x720 and the "before" was seen at 1920x1080.

| Fault | Cause | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** | `{Spheres}` ItemTemplate root was `HeightSizePolicy="Fixed" SuggestedHeight="40"`, sized for one `DiRealm.Value` line plus one `DiRealm.Small` line. `@VassalsText` is an enumeration of **every court in the rival sphere** and wrapped to six lines | **120px of text inside a 40px card, 79px of overflow.** The overflow printed over the `@SpheresTitleText` heading and over `@SphereGapNote`. Story hypothesis (a) was right |
| **F2** | `{ClaimGroups}` ItemTemplate root was `HeightSizePolicy="CoverChildren"` with a `StretchToParent` backdrop and a `StretchToParent` accent bar - UI-INTEGRATION §0b.1 exactly | **538px card, 79px of content, 459px of blank.** The accent bar's run was measured by scanning for its colour: y=446 to y=984. Story hypothesis (b) was right |

F3 was F2: the claims card's 538px set the row's height, which set the scroll length, and left the other two columns beside empty space | Story hypothesis (c) was **wrong**. ### 9b. ST-2 — the shape

**Keep three columns. Change no arrangement.** F3 turned out to be F2, so D1's escape hatches

### 9c. ST-3 — the card convention, and the one thing Gauntlet will not do

The fix is that **a card's background becomes the card's own `Brush`, on a `ListPanel`, instead
of a `StretchToParent` child.** Three measured facts forced it:

1. **A plain `Widget` with `HeightSizePolicy="CoverChildren"` measures its children, but its
   backdrop must then be a `StretchToParent` child** - which is §0b.1, the original fault. There
   is no way to paint a `CoverChildren` plain `Widget`'s own background.
2. **A `ListPanel` with `CoverChildren` does measure its children**, and a `ListPanel` paints its
   own `Brush`. This is the only container that satisfies R2's "sized by its content" *and* R1.
3. An intermediate attempt converted all ten cards at once to `Widget CoverChildren` and **the
   entire scroll body collapsed to nothing** - the mod log stayed clean, which is the §0b "six
   cases that rendered wrong with no error" again. Reverted, and the cards were converted in
   batches of three so a mistake could be attributed.

This needs **five brushes that did not exist**: `DiRealm.Card`, `CardDark`, `CardFaint`,
`CardStrip`, `CardRow`. D1 says no new brushes without asking. The colours are **byte-identical**
to the `Color=` attributes already in the file (`#201A1388`, `#1D171188`, `#1A161088`, `#24201888`,
`#201A13CC`) - no new colour is introduced and AC3's visual output is unchanged. It is the only
way found to let a card's background follow wrapped, translated text. **This is D4 and it needs
the lead's confirmation before merge.**

**The accent stripe cannot be done, and that is D5.** It is a `Fixed`-width widget with
`Color="@AccentColor"`, so it must be a widget rather than a brush, and it must match the card's
height. Measured, on v1.5.3:

- a `StretchToParent` child of a `CoverChildren` parent is given the **available** height, not
  the measured one - about **350px of stripe down a 100px card**;
- `VerticalAlignment` has only `Top`, `Center`, `Bottom` (ApiDump) - there is no `Stretch`;
- `ClipContents="true"` trimmed **neither** a `ListPanel` nor a plain `Widget` wrapper around it;
- a tall `Fixed` stripe cannot be used instead, because a `Fixed` child contributes its full
  height to a `CoverChildren` parent's measurement and would make the card 1200px tall.

So a full-height accent stripe in a bound colour is not expressible on a content-sized card in
Gauntlet. Left as it was rather than guessed at. This is a product decision, not a technical one.

### 9f. ST-4 attempt, reverted: converting the four remaining `Fixed` cards broke the panel

Told to finish the job, the four `Fixed` cards were converted in one pass - the standing strip
(84), `{Wars}` (74), `{Vassals}` (244), `{Agreements}` (64) - plus the three counter-intelligence
label/value rows that were `Fixed` at 24px over a label which wraps in German. The XML parsed and
the build was clean. **On screen it regressed**: on `di_offices_test` only the first of the two war
cards drew, and nothing below the wars strip rendered at all, though the scrollbar reported about
two and a half screens of content. Mod log clean, as always.

**Not diagnosed, and that is why it is written down.** The war card was the prime suspect: its root
became `CoverChildren` and its accent bar is a `StretchToParent` child, which is the exact pair this
story has already measured to stretch (§0b.1). If the accent drives the card's height there, the
card is a viewport tall, the second war card and everything below fall outside the fold, and the
strip would look exactly as it did. **That is a hypothesis, not a finding.** It was not tested,
because the session had no room left to bisect four cards against each other, and a half-understood
layout change is worse than none.

**Reverted to `36c6c8c`**, the last state verified on screen: two war cards, the claims column, six
Statecraft rows at a 24-25px pitch, the counter-intelligence heading, mod log clean (screenshots
22:05 and 22:21 on `di_offices_test`).

The lesson for the next attempt is **not** "convert four cards at once": the war card goes on its
own, with a screenshot after each step, and **D5 has to be settled first** - until the accent bar's
height is, a content-sized card that still holds a stretching child is a shape whose measured
behaviour is unknown, which is what the four converted cards turned out to be.

### 9g. ST-4 after D4 and D5: the cards inside the columns, and the two D5 leaves behind

With D4 approved and D5 settled as (a), the conversion was redone **only where the accent bar has

| Converted (verified live) | Left `Fixed`, and why |
|---|---|

**This is the reading of §9f, and it fits everything measured.** A `StretchToParent` accent inside a

accepts. As a direct child of the scroll list there is no ceiling, the card inflates, and everything
below it falls outside the fold. That is why §9f's four-card pass failed and this one does not.
**It is an explanation fitted to the evidence, not a measurement** - the ceiling itself was never
measured directly.

### 9e. A trap worth writing down: staging a ruler raises a notification that will not go away

`StatecraftVM.Show` requires `realm.IsRealm()`, so **the Statecraft block never draws for a
vassal** - every vassal run so far could not have seen it, this one included. Staging a ruler with
`diplomacy.test_player_rule` raises the `ChangeKingdomAction` scene notification, and:

- `ui/answer_inquiry` clears the **first** item only (`dismissed_JoinKingdomSceneNotificationItem`);
- the rest sit in a "Zum Fortfahren klicken" phase that reports **no active inquiry**, so the bridge
  cannot dismiss it;
- synthetic `mouse_event` clicks are ignored - the game reads input through its own path;
- and **pushing the Kingdom screen re-raises it**, so it comes back over the panel every time.

Draining it needs a real click on the game's own window. The way round it is a save where the player
already rules: **`di_offices_test`** does this with no notification at all, and it is the save to use
for Statecraft and for any other ruler-only surface. Two sessions were lost to this on 2026-10-04.

## 1. User story

> **As** a player who rules or serves a realm,
> **I want** the Realm tab to show my standing, wars, claims and agreements without any text
> drawn over other text,
> **so that** I can read what a treaty or a claim is worth without hunting for it.

> **As** a player who reads the game in German or Russian,
> **I want** the same tab to hold together when every label is a third longer,
> **so that** a translation does not turn the tab into a pile of overlapping lines.

## 2. What the lead saw (2026-10-04)

The lead's words: some regions of text overlap, the player has to scroll a lot, and there is a lot of
empty space. The screenshots show three separate faults, and they should not be fixed as one:

| # | Fault | Where | Seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Text drawn over text.** The "Spheres on the map" heading and its explanation run into the block below it, so two paragraphs print on the same lines | Left column, under our sphere | English, 1920x1080 |
| F2 | **A tall empty stretch inside the claims column.** The first claim group is followed by a blank area several hundred pixels high, and the next group (Battania's claims) sits at the very bottom, reached only by scrolling | Middle column, after the first group | Deutsch, scrolled; the first view of the English tab shows the same column shorter |
| F3 | **Scrolling while most of the panel is empty.** The three columns are different heights, so the longest one sets the scroll length and the other two leave blank space beside it | Whole tab | Both |

A fourth item the lead named in an earlier pass (TODO, "Kingdom-UI loose ends"): the Realm tab
**widens the tab strip**. It is in scope only if ST-1 shows it shares a cause with F1-F3.

## 3. Scope

**In**
- The Realm tab's prefab ([DiRealmPanel.xml](../../module/DiplomacyIntrigue/GUI/Prefabs/KingdomManagement/DiRealmPanel.xml),
  867 lines) and the view-model properties that feed its text ([RealmVM.cs](../../src/DiplomacyIntrigue/UI/KingdomScreen/RealmVM.cs)).
- Fixing F1, F2, F3 in English first, then checking German (`DE`), Russian (`RU`) and Simplified
  Chinese (`CNs`) with the fixture procedure of story 4.1 §6a.
- The tab-strip widening, if it is the same fault.
- The Court tab's own overlap ("Make amends" over the loyalty line), as ST-6: the same kind of fault
  with the same cure, decided in (D2).

**Out**
- Any change to what the tab says or to a number it shows (R5 of story 4.1 still holds: the number on
  screen is the number the AI used).
- Re-designing the Realm tab. The approved mockup stays the reference for what is shown and in what
  order; D1 says how far the arrangement may move.
- New sprites or brushes; the tab already builds cards from `BlankWhiteSquare_9` and a colour.

## 4. Business rules

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | **No text over text.** At every size the panel is shown at, no `TextWidget` overlaps another. A line that does not fit wraps or is cut by its own card; it never prints on a neighbour |
| R2 | **Height follows content, or is fixed on purpose.** A card is either `Fixed` height with its text proven to fit, or sized by its content. A `CoverChildren` widget holding a `StretchToParent` child is not allowed (UI-INTEGRATION §0b.1, §0b.4: this bit the same panel twice) |
| R3 | **A column never sets the height of its neighbours' blank space.** Where one column is much taller, the shorter ones are not left holding an empty strip next to it. How that is achieved is ST-2's decision |
| R4 | **Longest language is the test, not English.** A fix that holds only in English is not done. English is the shortest of the 13 |
| R5 | **Text is not shortened to make it fit.** Words are the lead's and the translators'; the layout makes room. (If a line truly cannot be fitted, report it; do not cut it.) |
| R6 | **No change to data or logic.** Same properties, same text, same order of information |

## 5. Acceptance criteria

**AC1 — No overlap**
- On `di_phase1_full` and one save with a vassal, a sphere and a live claim, no text overlaps another in
  the Realm tab, in English, `DE`, `RU` and `CNs`. Evidence is a screenshot of each, not a statement.

**AC2 — No wasted scroll**
- With the content of AC1 the tab needs no more scrolling than its longest column requires, and no
  blank area taller than one card sits between two blocks of content. Stated as a measured number in
  ST-1 and re-measured in ST-5.

**AC3 — Content unchanged**
- Every property the prefab bound before it binds after; every sentence reads as before (English
  character for character). `check-localization.ps1` passes, and the 4.1 golden file is unchanged.

**AC4 — Nothing else moves**
- The Diplomacy and Court tabs, the vanilla tabs and the tab strip render as before. If the strip
  widening is fixed, that is shown by a screenshot of the strip.

**AC5 — Honest about what was exercised**
- Each AC marked verified live, compile only, or not verified. Anything not clicked says so.

**How to look.** `diplomacy.test_player_join <kingdom>`, dismiss the join notice with
`ui.answer_inquiry`, then open the Kingdom screen (since 4.1 the `test_open_kingdom` command builds the
screen outside the English scope, so a language check on it is real). Click `Realm`. A screenshot taken
right after a click can be one frame old: take it twice, or read `ui.get_screen`'s `Selected` state first.
The bridge cannot scroll, so a fault that appears only below the fold is seen by lengthening the panel in
a test save or by reading widget sizes, and the story records which.

## 7. Decisions

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | May the layout depart from the approved mockup? | **Yes, within limits (tech lead, 2026-10-04, delegated by the lead).** The mockup fixes *what is shown, in what order, and how a card looks* (colours, accent bars, type brushes, the standing strip and wars strip on top). It does **not** fix three columns of equal rank: if the columns cannot be made to size themselves, the dev may stack blocks full-width or use two columns. Not allowed without asking: dropping or merging a block, moving a block above the wars strip, new colours or brushes, changing any text. The dev attaches a before/after screenshot pair to the pull request, and the lead looks at it before merge |
| D2 | Is the Court tab's overlap part of this story? | **Yes, as ST-6, last and as its own commit.** It is the same fault family (a fixed-size or stretched widget over wrapped text), it is already in TODO beside this one, and doing it while the dev has the method fresh costs less than a second story. If ST-1 shows its cause is unrelated, split it out rather than letting it stall ST-1..5 |

**For the dev: where to start.** The Realm prefab's text bindings were rewritten by story 4.1 (labels moved
into view-model properties), and the check script that guards them is `scripts/check-localization.ps1`.
Branch from `feature/4.1-localization`, or from `development` once that has merged - not from an older
`development`, or the prefab will conflict. Read [UI-INTEGRATION.md](../UI-INTEGRATION.md) §0b before
touching a prefab, and CLAUDE.md §2 for the deploy-and-look loop (the game must be closed to deploy, and
`games_stop` does not always close it).
| D3 | Which resolutions? | 1920x1080 is the only one seen so far. Other sizes are checked if the machine can be set to them; otherwise the story says they were not |

## 8. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| A prefab edit renders wrong with no error (UI-INTEGRATION §0b: six such cases already) | Read §0b before editing; parse the XML before deploying; look at the screen, not at the build |
| The bug hides until something sits below it, as §0b.1 did | AC1 is checked on a save with every section present, not on the emptiest |
| Fixing English breaks German | R4, ST-5; English is checked first only because it is the quickest |
| The bridge cannot scroll, so a fault below the fold is missed | ST-1 measures widget sizes as well as taking screenshots |
| A `StretchToParent` that fixes one column re-breaks the row, as the column rules did | R2; every change is followed by a screenshot with all sections visible |

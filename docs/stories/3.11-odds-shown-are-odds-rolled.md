## 1. User story

> **As** a player launching an operation,
> **I want** to know that the odds I am shown at launch are today's odds and can change before the
> operation resolves,
> **so that** a result rolled at a different figure does not look like the game lied to me.

## 2. Why

Run 09: an operation launched at **68%** resolved with "success was **71%**". `Missions.Resolve`
calls `OddsOf` again on the due day, with the network's strength, the handler's skills and the
target's counter-intelligence **as they are then**. The "operations under way" list on the
Intelligence tab already shows the live figure
([IntelligenceVM.cs:480-486](../../src/DiplomacyIntrigue/UI/ClanScreen/IntelligenceVM.cs#L480-L486)),
but neither the planning overlay nor the result says that the figure moves.

Two ways to make the shown number the rolled number:

- **(a) The odds move, and the UI says so.** No save data. A target that raises its
  counter-intelligence budget after launch still defends against the operation, which is what the
  budget is for, and a network that keeps growing still helps.
- **(b) Freeze the odds at launch.** Two new `SpyMission` properties (13, 14), a fallback for older
  saves, and a defence that cannot touch an operation already under way.

**Decided: (a).** Freezing would make counter-intelligence spending useless against anything
already launched, which weakens the lever 3.3 built.

## 3. Business rules

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | The planning overlay, under the odds, says in one line: "Rolled on the day it resolves, at the odds then. Their counter-intelligence and our network's strength can change them" (final wording through `design:ux-copy` or the lead) |
| R2 | The resolution notice names the figure as the day's: "success was 71% on the day". It does **not** quote the launch figure: the launch odds are not saved, and a save field only to print a comparison is not worth a frozen id. The launch figure stays in the log's launch line, where a reader can compare the two |
| R3 | The under-way list keeps the live figure (unchanged), and says "now" after it: "71% now" |
| R4 | Nothing changes in how the roll is made |

## 4. Acceptance criteria

**AC1** — The overlay and the under-way row show the text of R1 and R3 (screenshot, via `test_intel`).
**AC2** — An operation launched, then `test_counter_budget` on the target and `test_network_week`,
then `test_resolve_mission` without a forced outcome: the notice reads as R2 says, and its figure is
the one the roll used (the log line).
**AC3** — `build.ps1` and `compile-check.sh` pass; `check-save-ids.ps1` unchanged.

## The headline finding: F3's gap is real and it swallowed the exact case it was built for

**Southern Empire was attacked four times in two years** (Vlandia 203d, Khuzait 213d, Aserai
42d, Sturgia 47d). Its patron Western Empire could not defend it in any of them — and the
system recorded **zero neglect** the entire time:

- `Protection()` skips a war when the patron holds a treaty forbidding war against the
  attacker (`HasTreatyForbiddingWar` → not `ignored++`). WE was bound to all of SE's
  attackers: tribute-paying to Vlandia *and* Khuzait, allied to Khuzait, def-pacted to
  Aserai. `protection` printed `+0.0` in every `[LINK]` line for two years.
- Hold therefore lived on trust (+15) and fear (±5) and never approached the defection
  line of 40. `Move.Defected` never had a chance to fire.
- Peak absurdity observed: **WE called Khuzait — the kingdom then eating its vassal — as
  its ally against Sturgia**, and Khuzait answered. A patron fighting *alongside* its
  vassal's predator, untouched by any ledger.
- Resolution: the vassalage lapsed at term (Hold 52 < renew 70). The patron paid nothing
  for two years of non-protection, then **declared `ReclaimAncestralLand` on its former
  vassal fourteen days after the bond lapsed**.

The run-06 F3 case was "patron blocked by truce/cascade while vassal dies". The implemented
fix gives the vassal an exit **only when Hold < 40** — but a patron can be treaty-legal
*and* relationship-healthy while completely failing to protect, which keeps Hold healthy and
the gate closed. The neglected-vassal scenario cannot produce a neglected *relationship*
under the current formula.

**Design call needed.** Options:

- (a) Count treaty-bound-to-attacker as fractional neglect in `Protection` — e.g.
  `ignored += 0.5`. The patron chose the binding treaty; the vassal still reads it as a
  protection failure, Hold erodes, and the F3 exit can open. Keeps "not called" honest
  while making "chose not to be callable" cost something.
- (b) Keep `Protection` as-is; add a direct Hold/trust hit when a patron *signs or holds*
  a pact/alliance/tribute with a kingdom currently attacking its vassal.
- (c) Accept it: legal neglect resolves only through lapse. (Not recommended — both run 06
  and this session show the gap eating exactly the scenario F3 was written for.)

## Second finding: the concession ladder still pays nothing

> **Correction (review, same day).** True of this session's seven endings, not of the ladder:
> run 06 imposed 15 tributary pacts and ceded 5 fortifications at the peace table
> (run-06-part1/part3 analyses), and the evening check saw Khuzait made to pay Aserai tribute.

All seven war endings in the window were **white peace** (2 PeaceTable, 5 Dormant),
including Khuzait's 213-day, +23.2-score war on SE. The mechanism explains itself:

- The loser's listen threshold is exhaustion-only (SE: "starts listening at 44.1"). By the
  time it is reached, the *winner* is usually shattered too (Khuzait 70, Sturgia 61) and
  takes the white peace.
- War score buys almost nothing at real margins: Khuzait held ~25-32 for months, enough
  for a castle at 25 — yet no demand ever landed. Either the winner's AI never offered
  (unverifiable — refused offers are not logged) or the loser's acceptance math refused.

Open question for run 07: **should losing-score feed the listen threshold?** A kingdom at
-30 and exhaustion 25 knows it is losing; requiring exhaustion 44 turns every won war into
a grind to mutual collapse. Alternatively, demand cheaper confirmation of fiefs already
taken by siege — Khuzait's only war prize (Tamnuh Castle) existed purely because an army
took it, not because the table confirmed it.

## Third: tribute re-imposition is robotic

Vlandia re-imposed tributary pacts on Aserai and WE **on the exact expiry day**, every
cycle (Spring 1, Autumn 16 — the Khuzait-WE pact lapsed Autumn 10 and was re-signed Autumn
16). Correct by value, lifeless in play. A short cooldown or jitter, or letting a payer
refuse (becoming the patron's casus belli), would make it breathe.

## Confirmed not-bugs (checked in code during the session)

- `wars=0` in Hold terms while SE fought 3 wars: `WarBurden` counts only `IsObligationWar`
  — wars the vassal fights *for the patron*. Defensive wars are not patron obligations; the
  term is correct as designed.
- Aserai's `ourSupport=15330` when declaring on SE: it was Sturgia's strength via the
  Aserai-Sturgia **Alliance** (which obliges offensive calls), and Sturgia did join — its
  47-day dormant war on SE. The DefensivePact with WE correctly did not count
  (`Applies`: defensive-only + aggressor → false).
